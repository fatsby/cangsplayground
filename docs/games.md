# Games

| Game                | Players per group          | What happens                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free Talk Timer     | 2+ (3–8 recommended)       | A topic or question, timed speaking turns in each group                                                                                            |
| Secret Word         | 3+ (3–6 recommended)       | Everyone gets a secret word to work into what they say; the group tries to find it                                                                 |
| Who's the Spy       | 3+                         | Talk about a hidden topic without naming it; find the spy. Categories, spies per group, game and turn time are configurable                        |
| Shark Tank          | 2+                         | Prepare, pitch a wild product, ask questions, then huddle and vote with a budget. Prep, pitch, question and vote times and prices are configurable |
| Murder Mystery      | 3–4                        | B1 teams share private clues, choose four investigations and submit one evidence-backed accusation; one case and clock for the whole class         |
| Guess the Character | 1+ (even number of groups) | Two teams ask questions in English to guess each other's secret character                                                                          |

## Guess the Character

Groups play in pairs, all at the same time. Make an **even number of groups** (at least 2); a group can have a single
player and the two groups in a pair can differ in size. Each round, every pair votes for one of nine categories, then
each team gets a different secret character (hold the card to see it). Teams take turns asking; inside each team the
members rotate. One **turn time** (default 120 s) covers a question and its answer, and only the current questioner
can tap **I'm done** to end it early. Anyone in a pair can type a guess at any time; a wrong guess locks the whole team
for the **wrong guess cooldown** (default 5 s). The host can pause, skip or end each match, and starts a New round
once every match is over. Vote, turn and cooldown times are set before Prepare; timers run on the server.

## Murder Mystery

A cooperative mystery for **B1 English**, with **3–4 students per team**. One team or any number
of teams can play; every team investigates the same case and sees the solution together. Allow
about **24 minutes**. Keep phones private while sharing discoveries aloud.

1. Form teams and select **Murder Mystery → Prepare**. Teams must have 3–4 members when the case
   is prepared. The host can inspect the complete case privately; keep the host screen off the projector.
2. Tap **Start** for a silent **13-second animated intro** on the projector. Phones say “Watch the
   projector.” **Pause/Resume** freezes/resumes the intro; **Skip intro** opens the briefing immediately.
3. Read the public case file (**2 minutes**): four suspects, three possible weapons and four rooms.
   The place where the victim was found is not necessarily the crime room.
4. Share the four starting clues (**3 minutes**). Hold your card to read it and release to conceal it.
   Each student in a four-person team owns one card; a three-person team receives two, one and one.
   Read or explain your clue aloud and ask teammates what their clues suggest. **Unverified** NPC
   testimony can be wrong; **Confirmed** findings establish facts. Optional language help and Vietnamese
   vocabulary support the discussion; **Hide** remembers your preference on that device.
5. Discuss and choose **four investigations**, each with a **3-minute** class timer. The lead rotates
   once per round (A→B→C→D, or A→B→C→A for three students). Only the lead confirms the team's choice.
   Ask about an alibi or relationship, inspect an object/document, or **Challenge with evidence** when
   the matching NPC/topic/evidence option is available. Confirm the displayed target, question and
   evidence. Each action gives the lead 1–2 new private clues to tell the team. Other members see what
   was investigated, without receiving the private result text.
6. Agree on the culprit, weapon and crime room (**4 minutes**). All discovered team clues become
   readable to the team. The designated submitter (round four's lead) selects answers and **up to two
   discovered clues per answer**, uses the numbered clue references to match each evidence button to its card, taps **Save draft**, then **Review and submit → Yes, submit**.
   The team gets one confirmed submission; the deadline closes editing without submitting a draft.
7. After time expires or the host advances, everyone waits. Only the host's **Reveal** opens the
   solution and scores. The projector stages the solution story and automatically changes to the
   scoreboard after roughly 18 seconds; phones have **Result / Solution / Scoreboard** tabs sharing
   the same timeline. Reduced-motion mode shows the final frames immediately. Allow about three
   minutes to explain the evidence, then select **New case**.

Scoring: **culprit 3 + weapon 2 + room 2 + one point for each valid evidence selection = 10**.
Evidence must actually prove the corresponding correct answer from the public rules and confirmed
findings; an unsupported claim is not proof. Missing submissions score zero. There are no speed
points, and tied teams share rank.

The teacher controls the class clock. **Pause** locks student actions; **+30s** extends a timed phase,
including while paused. **Next → Yes, move on** skips remaining time and warns about teams losing an
unused investigation or an unsubmitted answer. Intro has Pause/Resume and Skip only. Reveal is always
manual. Phase settings range from 30–600 seconds in 30-second steps; four investigation rounds and
the intro duration remain fixed.

If a lead disconnects, pause or add time as needed. In that team's host panel, choose **Move from**
and a connected teammate under **Move to**, then **Reassign**. Their current/future leads and private
cards move for the rest of the case without spending an investigation. Reloading with the same saved
player identity restores current ownership and conceals the cards again. Removed players' cards go
to remaining teammates; a team reduced below three can finish with a warning. A new or same-name
rejoining player waits until the next case and does not inherit the old identity's cards. An empty team
cannot block the class. **Exit** cancels a case; New case is available only after Reveal and checks team
sizes again.

The app encourages English through information sharing, clarification and disagreement; it does not
record or assess speech. A **real B1 classroom playtest is still required** to judge whether everyone
contributes, whether the vocabulary works and whether the approximately 24-minute pacing suits the
class. Browser acceptance verifies controls and privacy, not spoken participation. See the
[implementation and acceptance report](superpowers/reports/2026-11-03-murder-mystery-result.md).
